About

I currently work as a Group Internal Audit Officer (Intern) at Botswana Development Corporation (BDC), supporting audit engagements, risk assessments, and control testing across the Corporation's operations.

I hold a BSc (Hons) in Finance and Accounting from the Institute of Development Management (IDM), and I'm a member of both the Botswana Institute of Chartered Accountants (BICA) and the Global Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA).

I enjoy the parts of finance that other people find tedious: making sure a set of records is accurate, a reconciliation ties out, and a report is ready before the deadline — and I'm actively looking to put that toward flexible, part-time, and international opportunities.
